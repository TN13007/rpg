# Game Vision — Adventurer Academy

**Status:** Current product direction  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-20

## High concept

A fantasy RPG about building a life, relationships and identity in an adventurer academy while taking on dangerous expeditions. Combat is the primary gameplay activity; the Academy supports preparation, character development and personal connections. A deeper narrative gives meaning to the player's choices and the people they meet.

## Experience priorities

1. **Combat first:** the central repeatable activity is challenging, expressive, party-based tactical combat.
2. **Character growth:** victories, training, equipment and skills expand what the party can do, not only inflate numerical stats.
3. **Narrative depth:** adventures and relationships contribute to a larger story with meaningful consequences.
4. **Academy as a supporting hub:** preparation, recovery, training, recruitment and character interaction support the main adventure loop.
5. **Personal freedom:** optional activities and personal-interest content do not become mandatory barriers to the central experience.

## Core pillars

### Tactical Timeline Combat

The player commands a four-person party and manipulates the order and timing of actions. The challenge comes from reading threats, choosing actions and coordinating party members.

### Meaningful Party Synergy

Each party member has a distinct identity and tactical contribution. Builds and skills create multiple viable combinations rather than mandatory fixed classes.

### Adventure and Consequence

Expeditions provide encounters, discoveries, rewards and story developments. The world reacts to meaningful successes, failures and decisions.

### Character and Narrative Investment

The Academy and its people give the player reasons to care about the party and the world beyond winning the next battle.

## Primary gameplay loop

```text
Academy
  Prepare the party, train, recover, interact and choose an expedition
      |
      v
Adventure
  Explore, discover objectives, encounter enemies and make decisions
      |
      v
Combat and events
  Fight, resolve threats, protect allies and complete encounter objectives
      |
      v
Consequences and rewards
  Gain resources, equipment, knowledge, story changes and character growth
      |
      v
Return to Academy
  Recover, develop builds, deepen relationships and prepare again
```

This is a player-experience loop, not a technical state machine or a mandatory sequence for every mission.

## Academy and adventure relationship

**Accepted:** Hybrid structure.

- The Academy is the recurring hub for preparation, training, recovery, recruitment and social interaction.
- Adventure areas are focused spaces for exploration, combat, objectives and story events.
- The player alternates between the hub and adventures; neither should feel like a disconnected game mode.

Academy activities should serve preparation, recovery, growth or narrative. Avoid expanding daily-life simulation or schedules unless they create meaningful player decisions.

## Content priority

- Prove combat and a complete first adventure loop before expanding breadth.
- Let character progression support combat and party identity.
- Develop narrative alongside playable content, without delaying proof of the core combat experience.
- Keep NSFW and other personal-interest content optional and secondary, not a core gameplay pillar or progression requirement.

## Scope test

Evaluate a proposed feature by asking:
- Does it strengthen combat, progression, adventure or narrative?
- Does it create a meaningful player decision?
- Can its value be demonstrated in a small playable slice?
- Is that value worth its development and maintenance cost?

If not, defer it.
