# Game Vision — Adventurer Academy RPG

**Status:** Current product direction  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-20

## High concept

A fantasy RPG about building a life, relationships and identity in an adventurer academy while taking on dangerous expeditions. Combat is the primary gameplay activity; the academy supports preparation, character development and personal connections. A deeper narrative gives meaning to the player's choices and the people they meet.

## Experience priorities

1. **Combat first:** the central repeatable activity should be challenging, expressive and rewarding party-based tactical combat.
2. **Character growth:** victories, training, equipment and skills should expand what the party can do, not only inflate numerical stats.
3. **Narrative depth:** the player's adventures and relationships should contribute to a larger story with meaningful consequences.
4. **Academy as a supporting hub:** the academy provides preparation, recovery, training, recruitment and character interaction. It must not overwhelm the combat loop with unrelated management.
5. **Personal freedom:** optional activities and personal content may exist, but they must not become mandatory barriers to the central experience.

## Core pillars

### Tactical Timeline Combat

The player commands a four-person party and manipulates the order and timing of actions. The challenge comes from reading threats, choosing actions and coordinating party members.

### Meaningful Party Synergy

Each party member has a distinct identity and tactical contribution. Builds and skills should create multiple viable combinations rather than mandatory fixed classes.

### Adventure and Consequence

Expeditions provide encounters, discoveries, rewards and story developments. The world should react to meaningful successes, failures and decisions.

### Character and Narrative Investment

The academy and its people give the player reasons to care about the party and the world beyond winning the next battle.

## World structure

**Accepted:** Hybrid structure.

- The Academy is the recurring hub for preparation, training, recovery, recruitment and social interaction.
- Adventure areas are dedicated spaces for exploration, combat, objectives and story events.
- The player alternates between the hub and adventures; neither should be a disconnected game mode.

## Content priority

- Combat and the first complete adventure loop are the first priority.
- Character progression and party identity support combat.
- Academy activities should directly support preparation, recovery, growth or narrative.
- Narrative should be developed alongside playable content, but should not delay proving the core combat experience.
- NSFW or other personal-interest content is optional secondary content, not a core gameplay pillar or a requirement for progression.

## Scope discipline

This is the product direction, not a promise that every feature is already implemented. A proposed feature should be evaluated by asking:
- Does it strengthen combat, progression, adventure or narrative?
- Does it create a meaningful player decision?
- Does it justify its development and maintenance cost?
- Can the experience be demonstrated in a small playable slice?

If not, defer it.
