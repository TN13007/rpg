# Product Roadmap

**Status:** Directional product sequence  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-20

This roadmap is organized around player-facing outcomes. It is not an implementation task list; session status, Issues, PRs and blockers remain in [WORKFLOW.md](../../WORKFLOW.md).

## Phase 1 — Prove the core combat

Deliver a complete, understandable encounter with a four-person party, visible Timeline, full player control, distinct enemy behaviors and a meaningful victory/defeat outcome.

**Exit criteria**
- The player can read the Timeline and anticipate the next actions.
- Party members have distinct, useful combat contributions.
- Timeline manipulation changes encounter outcomes.
- Enemy actions create meaningful pressure.
- Downed and revival are understandable and consequential.
- The encounter is challenging without relying on inflated HP or opaque rules.

## Phase 2 — Prove party progression

Deliver a short progression loop in which rewards and development choices change how the party fights.

**Exit criteria**
- At least two meaningful build directions are viable.
- Equipment or skills change tactical decisions, not only damage values.
- The player can identify and understand the benefit of each growth choice.

## Phase 3 — Prove the adventure loop

Deliver a compact adventure that connects preparation, exploration, multiple encounters, resource decisions, objectives and rewards.

**Exit criteria**
- The player has a reason to enter the area.
- Encounters vary in tactical problem.
- Resources create decisions between encounters.
- The adventure has a satisfying end and return to the Academy.

## Phase 4 — Make the Academy matter

Deliver a focused hub experience for preparation, training, recovery, recruitment and selected character interactions.

**Exit criteria**
- Academy activities support the next adventure or deepen investment in characters.
- The player understands the value of spending time at the hub.
- Daily-life systems do not overwhelm the main adventure loop.

## Phase 5 — Deliver the first complete narrative arc

Connect the combat/adventure loop to a story with meaningful character motivations, choices and consequences.

**Exit criteria**
- The player can explain the central conflict and personal stakes.
- Major choices produce observable consequences.
- NPCs and locations reflect important events.
- The arc has a coherent beginning, escalation and resolution.

## Phase 6 — Expand the world

Add further areas, enemy families, party members, narrative arcs and long-term world challenges based on evidence from the earlier phases.

Do not expand breadth before the core loop is satisfying.

## Scope rule

A phase is complete when its player-facing exit criteria can be demonstrated in a playable build. A document, architecture layer or collection of implemented systems is not by itself proof of a complete player experience.
